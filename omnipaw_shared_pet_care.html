The Hand-off Problem

Sticky notes get lost. Text messages are forgotten. When multiple people care for a pet, crucial routines slip through the cracks. Omnipaw eliminates the chaos of shared responsibility by acting as a single source of truth for your pet's life.

Seamless
Transitions.

Whether you are passing the leash to a dog sitter for the weekend, or sharing daily duties with your spouse, Omnipaw ensures everyone is on exactly the same page.

  • No more missed medications.
  • Instant access to emergency vet contacts.
  • Real-time feeding and walk logs.

Everything in One Place

A comprehensive suite of tools built specifically for collaborative pet parenting.

Health Schedules

Track vaccinations, flea/tick treatments, and set automated reminders that sync across all caregivers' devices.

Document Vault

Securely store adoption papers, insurance details, and medical records. Easily generate a sharing link for boarding facilities.

Daily Tracking

Log meals, walks, mood, and potty breaks. View historical data to spot behavioral changes or health issues early.
